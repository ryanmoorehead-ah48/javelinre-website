Speaking engagements

Looking for Ryan to speak at a conference, sit on a panel or join a podcast? He brings two decades of operating experience, from running the 12,271-unit Parkchester community to growing a NYC portfolio past 14,000 units, and the perspective of a proptech founder.

Topics

Each talk can be tailored to the audience: owners and investors, operators, public agencies, technologists or students.

Affordable housing operations at scale

How a NYC management company grew from 5,000 to 14,000+ units: staffing, systems, compliance and the decisions that held it together.

AI and technology in affordable housing

What AI can and can't do for leasing, income certification and compliance, from the co-founder of an affordable housing leasing platform.

Value-add in occupied buildings

Lessons from a $15M+ renovation program at Parkchester delivered $3M under budget, with residents in place.

Turning around a property organization

Consolidating fragmented management, writing the playbook, and building teams that own the hand-offs.

Building a career in real estate

From Sony Music to running a 12,271-unit community, and more than a decade coaching MBA students at Columbia.

Procurement and the cost of operations

Where operating budgets leak, and how owners recover it through vendors, staffing and capital planning.

Speaker bio

For programs and introductions

Ryan Moorehead is the Founder of Javelin Residential and co-founder of Arlo, an AI-powered leasing and compliance platform for affordable housing. As President of GRC Management from 2019 to 2025, he grew a New York City portfolio from 5,000 to more than 14,000 affordable and market-rate units. Previously, as Head of Residential at Olshan Properties, he was CEO of Parkchester, the 12,271-unit Bronx community, where he delivered a $15M+ renovation program $3M under budget. A GlobeSt.com Top 50 Under 40 honoree, he has been a real estate career coach for Columbia University since 2013 and holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and a B.S. in Real Estate from NYU.

  • Has spoken at REEC, DCRE, AAREPNY and CRETech
  • GlobeSt.com Top 50 Under 40 (2016)
  • Columbia University career coach since 2013
  • Member, REEC, NYREC and ULI

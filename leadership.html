Ryan Moorehead

Founder & Principal, Javelin Residential. Real estate operator, company builder and, since 2013, a real estate career coach at Columbia Business School.

Experience20+ years in real estate
EducationColumbia MBA · NYU B.S.
Portfolio14,000+ units grown at GRC

Ryan Moorehead has spent more than twenty years in residential real estate, across development, asset management, property management and technology. He founded Javelin Residential to give owners what he wanted as an operator: one accountable partner across management, construction, procurement and people.

His path into real estate was not a straight line. Ryan grew up in Oakland, California, and started college at UC Berkeley, where he played football. He left to study music theory and sound design in Arizona, then began his career at Sony Music, working his way from intern to signing an artist from his hometown while taking classes at NYU at night. It was at NYU that he found real estate. He moved to The Richman Group, where he developed a lasting interest in affordable housing, and interned at AvalonBay while in graduate school. He graduated from NYU with a B.S. in Real Estate, magna cum laude, and holds an MBA from Columbia Business School.

Asset management and turnarounds

From 2010 to 2012, as Vice President of Asset Management and Business Development at First Sterling Financial (now Regents Bank), Ryan grew the firm by turning its asset management platform into a third-party management business, while managing risk across an asset-based portfolio with a focus on cost control. He was instrumental in loan negotiations that reduced the number of non-performing assets in the portfolio. He also consulted for workforce housing firms, building their property and asset management reporting packages and standard operating procedures.

Parkchester

From 2012 to 2017, Ryan was Head of Residential for Olshan Properties, a privately owned New York real estate firm with a $2 billion multifamily portfolio. In that role he was CEO of Parkchester in the Bronx, a 12,271-unit development with two condominium boards and 500,000 square feet of retail. He created a modernization program and oversaw more than $15 million in apartment renovations, finishing $3 million under budget while lifting revenue more than 12 percent and ROI by 20.6 percent. His value-add strategies, operational efficiencies and organizational changes grew ownership's profits by 60 percent, and he led the complex, sponsor-led refinancing of Parkchester, Olshan's premier residential asset, while managing teams across multiple states.

The most valuable fixes in a building are usually organizational: who owns the hand-off, who signs the contract, who checks the file.

GRC Management

From April 2019 to October 2025, Ryan served as President of GRC Management, a 49-year-old full-service residential property management company in New York City, which he partnered with to restructure and modernize. He grew its portfolio from 5,000 to more than 14,000 units of affordable and market-rate housing, with more than 400 employees across the portfolio. That work, rebuilding staffing, systems, compliance and reporting at scale, is the foundation of Javelin's practice today. He stepped away from GRC in October 2025 to focus on Javelin and Arlo.

Building companies

Alongside Javelin, Ryan is co-founder of Arlo, an AI-powered leasing and compliance platform for affordable housing. He also owns Affogato House, a specialty coffee and gelato café in New Rochelle, New York, which he took from raw space through design, construction and opening. Outside of real estate, he is a published poet with two collections in print.

Recognition, coaching and community

GlobeSt.com named Ryan to its Top 50 Under 40 at the 2016 Real Estate Forum. Since 2013 he has been a real estate career coach for Columbia University, working with MBA students and alumni moving into or within real estate, and he now offers one-on-one coaching through Javelin. He is a member of REEC, NYREC and ULI, and has spoken at REEC, DCRE, AAREPNY and CRETech on affordable housing operations, real estate technology and careers.

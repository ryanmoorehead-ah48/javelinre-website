Asset management and affordable housing compliance

Owner-side oversight that protects value, financing and regulatory standing, offered as a monthly retainer or as defined projects such as pre-audit file reviews.

Book a 15-minute callSend an RFP or scope

Affordable housing owners answer to lenders, investors, syndicators and agencies at the same time. A missed recertification or an incomplete move-in file can put tax credits, subsidy and refinancing at risk. Javelin acts as the owner's asset manager: we watch the property's performance and its compliance, and we hold the managing agent to a standard.

Engagements usually start with a file and operations review that shows exactly where a property stands before an audit, a Management and Occupancy Review or a refinancing, followed by a plan to close every gap.

What's included

  • LIHTC and HUD compliance oversight
  • Recertification tracking and file systems
  • Pre-audit move-in and recertification file reviews
  • Management and Occupancy Review (MOR) preparation
  • Budgets, variance reporting and capital planning
  • Due diligence for acquisitions and refinancings

What you get

  • Audit-ready files and a recertification calendar
  • A clear findings list with owners and dates
  • Fewer surprises at MORs and investor reviews
  • Monthly reporting that ties operations to the asset
  • A managing agent held to agreed standards

Who it's for

Nonprofit and for-profit owners of affordable housing, syndicators and asset managers who need fractional, senior oversight without a full-time hire.

Questions

Do you offer one-time file reviews?

Yes. Pre-audit file reviews and MOR preparation are fixed-scope projects with a findings report and a corrective action plan.

What does a monthly retainer cover?

Ongoing compliance oversight, monthly reporting review, budget and variance review, and regular calls with the owner and managing agent, at a flat monthly fee.

Do you work alongside our current managing agent?

Usually, yes. We work on the owner's side and with the agent, not instead of it, unless you want management to change.

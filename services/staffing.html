Staffing for property management teams

The right people in the right seats, from property managers and leasing agents to compliance specialists and senior leadership, including interim coverage while you hire.

Book a 15-minute callSend an RFP or scope

Vacancies in a site team show up quickly as vacant units, late recertifications and unanswered work orders. Javelin builds staffing plans that match roles to the work each property actually needs, recruits for those roles, and covers critical seats on an interim basis so operations don't stall.

We recruit from deep experience in New York's affordable and multifamily housing sector and screen for the compliance knowledge regulated properties require.

What's included

  • Staffing plans and role design
  • Recruiting and placement
  • Interim property and compliance managers
  • Fractional senior leadership
  • Job descriptions and interview guides
  • Onboarding and training plans

What you get

  • A staffing plan matched to each property
  • Critical vacancies covered quickly
  • Candidates screened for compliance knowledge
  • Clear job descriptions and reporting lines
  • New hires productive sooner

Who it's for

Owners and management companies with open site or leadership roles, teams growing into new properties, and organizations restructuring their staffing.

Questions

Which roles do you staff?

Property managers, community and leasing staff, compliance specialists, asset managers and senior operations leaders.

Can you cover a role while we hire?

Yes. Interim and fractional coverage keeps recertifications, leasing and reporting moving.

Do you also design the org chart?

Yes. See Organizational Management for structure, policies and reporting lines.

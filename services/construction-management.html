Construction management and owner's representation

We sit on the owner's side of the table on capital projects, so the scope is right, the bids compare cleanly and the job closes on time and on budget.

Book a 15-minute callSend an RFP or scope

Capital projects in occupied residential buildings carry risks that ground-up construction doesn't: residents in place, regulatory agreements, agency inspections and tight budgets. Javelin manages those projects for owners, from the first scope through bidding, construction and closeout.

We write the scope so contractors price the same job, level the bids, negotiate the contract, and then run the meetings, change orders, pay applications and punch lists until the work is done.

What's included

  • Capital needs planning and budgeting
  • Scope of work and bid documents
  • Bid leveling and contractor selection
  • Contract negotiation
  • Schedule, budget and change-order control
  • Inspections, punch lists and closeout

What you get

  • Bids you can compare line by line
  • Change orders reviewed before they're approved
  • Pay applications tied to completed work
  • A project record ready for lenders and agencies
  • Resident disruption kept to a minimum

Who it's for

Owners with capital projects in occupied buildings: façade and roof work, systems replacements, common-area and unit renovations, and lender- or agency-required repairs.

Questions

Are you a general contractor?

On owner's-rep engagements we are not the contractor; we represent the owner and manage the contractors. For unit turns and renovations, see Construction & Renovation.

What size projects do you manage?

From single-building upgrades to multi-building capital programs.

When should we bring you in?

As early as possible: the scope and bid stage is where most cost overruns are prevented.

Property operations and NOI improvement

An operational assessment that finds where a property is losing money, followed by the hands-on work to recover it.

Book a 15-minute callSend an RFP or scope

Most underperforming properties don't need a new strategy. They need the basics run tightly: units turned and leased quickly, payroll matched to the work, service contracts priced at market and utilities managed. Javelin's operations work starts on site, with walkthroughs and file reviews, and ends with measurable changes to occupancy and expense.

We're particularly effective on properties with long-vacant units, where the delay is usually in the hand-offs between maintenance, leasing and compliance rather than in any single team.

What's included

  • On-site operational assessment
  • Vacancy and unit-turn pipeline review
  • Payroll and staffing efficiency
  • Service contract and utility review
  • Collections and arrears process
  • Implementation support and follow-up

What you get

  • A ranked list of revenue and cost fixes
  • Shorter vacancy and turn times
  • Lower controllable expenses
  • Clear owners and deadlines for each fix
  • A reporting cadence that holds the gains

Who it's for

Owners and managers with properties that are under budget, carrying long-vacant units, or preparing for a sale or refinancing.

Questions

How long does an assessment take?

Most single-property assessments take two to four weeks, including site visits and file reviews, and end with a written plan.

Do you implement the plan or just recommend it?

Both. Many clients have us implement the plan through our construction, procurement and staffing services.

What size properties do you work with?

From single buildings to multi-property portfolios with hundreds of units.

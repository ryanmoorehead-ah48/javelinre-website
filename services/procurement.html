Procurement and vendor management

Strategic sourcing that lowers operating costs and makes vendors accountable, from a single service contract to a portfolio-wide rebid.

Book a 15-minute callSend an RFP or scope

Service contracts are one of the largest controllable expenses in a residential building, and many are renewed for years without being tested against the market. Javelin audits what you're paying, rewrites scopes so vendors price the same work, runs competitive RFPs and negotiates the contracts.

We also set up the purchasing rules, approvals and vendor files that keep costs in line after the rebid, which matters to auditors, lenders and agencies as much as to the budget.

What's included

  • Service contract audits
  • Scopes and RFPs for elevators, waste, extermination, janitorial, security and more
  • Bid analysis and vendor selection
  • Contract negotiation and renewal terms
  • Materials and supply purchasing
  • Vendor compliance files and insurance tracking

What you get

  • Contracts priced against the market
  • Scopes with clear service levels
  • Clean vendor files for audits
  • Purchasing approvals that hold
  • Savings you can see in the operating budget

Who it's for

Owners and managers whose service contracts haven't been rebid in years, portfolios consolidating vendors, and organizations that need procurement policies.

Questions

Which contracts do you usually rebid first?

Elevator maintenance, waste, extermination, janitorial and security are common starting points because they recur and are often above market.

Can you write our procurement policy?

Yes. We write procurement and approval policies as part of our organizational management work.

Do you take a share of savings?

Engagements are usually a flat project fee. Ask about alternatives on an intro call.

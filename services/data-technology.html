Data, reporting and property technology

Reporting and technology that tie what happens in each building to the decisions owners make about the portfolio.

Book a 15-minute callSend an RFP or scope

Owners often have more data than insight: several systems, inconsistent reports and no single view of occupancy, collections, compliance and capital needs. Javelin builds portfolio reporting that answers the questions owners actually ask, and advises on technology for leasing, compliance and operations.

Our founder also co-founded Arlo, an AI-powered leasing and compliance platform for affordable housing, so our technology advice comes from building in this sector.

What's included

  • Portfolio dashboards and KPIs
  • Monthly and quarterly owner reporting
  • Operational and compliance risk assessments
  • PropTech evaluation and selection
  • System implementation planning
  • Data cleanup and migration

What you get

  • One view of portfolio performance
  • Reports built around decisions
  • Risks identified before they become findings
  • Technology chosen for fit, not features
  • Cleaner data across systems

Who it's for

Owners and asset managers with multiple properties or systems, and organizations choosing new leasing, compliance or property management software.

Questions

Do you sell software?

No. Our technology advice is independent. We help you choose what fits.

Can you build our owner reports?

Yes, from your existing systems, with the KPIs and format your investors and lenders expect.

Do you work with Yardi, RealPage or MRI?

We work from the data in whatever systems you use.

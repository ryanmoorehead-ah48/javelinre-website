Organizational management for housing organizations

Structure, process and accountability for management teams that need to scale, consolidate or pass an audit.

Book a 15-minute callSend an RFP or scope

Many operating problems are organizational: nobody owns the hand-off, policies live in one person's head, or two departments do the same work. Javelin designs the structure, writes the policies and procedures, and sets up the reporting that makes accountability visible.

This work draws directly on experience consolidating a large, fragmented management operation into a single structure, and on running a New York City property management company.

What's included

  • Organizational assessment
  • Org charts, roles and reporting lines
  • Policies and procedures manuals
  • Performance management and KPIs
  • Portfolio and department consolidation
  • Change management and training

What you get

  • Every function has a named owner
  • Written policies staff can follow
  • KPIs reviewed on a set cadence
  • Duplicate work removed
  • An organization ready to scale

Who it's for

Property management companies, nonprofit housing organizations and owners consolidating portfolios or preparing for growth, audits or new funding.

Questions

Can you write our policies and procedures manual?

Yes, covering leasing, compliance, maintenance, procurement, finance and HR procedures for property operations.

How do you handle staff during a restructuring?

We plan communication and training so staff understand the new structure and their role in it.

Is this a one-time project?

It can be. Many clients keep a monthly retainer afterward to review KPIs and adjust.

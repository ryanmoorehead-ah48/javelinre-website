Differentiators

  • One accountable team across operations, construction, procurement and staffing

Past performance

  • GRC Management, New York City (President, April 2019–October 2025): restructured and modernized a 49-year-old full-service management company; grew the portfolio from 5,000 to 14,000+ affordable and market-rate units with 400+ employees
  • Parkchester, Bronx (CEO, for Olshan Properties, 2012–2017): 12,271-unit development with two condominium boards and 500,000 sq. ft. of retail; $15M+ apartment renovation program completed $3M under budget, revenue up 12%, ROI up 20.6%, ownership profits up 60%; complex sponsor-led refinance
  • Nonprofit affordable housing owner, Newark, NJ (Javelin, 2026–present): asset management, staffing, policies and procedures, LIHTC compliance and recertifications
  • Harlem affordable housing developer (Javelin, 2026): managing-agent agency approval submission
  • Workforce housing firms (consultant): property and asset management reporting packages and standard operating procedures
  • First Sterling Financial, now Regents Bank (VP, Asset Management & Business Development, 2010–2012): built third-party management from the asset management platform; negotiated loans to reduce non-performing assets

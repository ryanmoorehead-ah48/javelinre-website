Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Who we are

Javelin Residential ("Javelin," "we," "us") operates www.javelinre.com. Our office is at 173 Huguenot Street, Suite 200, New Rochelle, NY 10801. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@javelinre.com.

Information you give us

When you use a form on this site (a general inquiry, an RFP or scope, a speaking request or a newsletter sign-up) we collect what you enter, such as your name, organization, email address, phone number and message. We use it only to respond to you, evaluate the request and keep a record of our correspondence.

Scheduling

Meetings are booked through Calendly. When you schedule a call, Calendly collects your name, email address and any answers you provide, and shares them with us so we can hold the meeting. Calendly's own privacy policy applies to the information it processes.

Analytics and advertising

We may use Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand how visitors find and use the site and to measure whether our advertising leads to inquiries or booked calls. These services use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and the website that referred you. They do not give us your name or contact details. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on and control ad personalization in your Google account settings.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Beyond analytics and advertising cookies, this site does not set cookies for its own purposes. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site and respond to you, such as our hosting provider (Vercel), email delivery service, Calendly and Google, and only as needed for those purposes, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and correspondence records for as long as needed to respond, carry out any resulting engagement and meet legal and accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or unsubscribe from any email at any time by writing to info@javelinre.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive personal or financial information through our forms.

Children

This site is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date below shows when it last changed.
